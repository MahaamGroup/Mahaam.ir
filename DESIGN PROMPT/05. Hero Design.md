# Mahaam — Hero Design

## Role

The hero establishes that Mahaam is a serious, distinctive decision-support organization. It must make the visitor feel oriented, not sold to.

## Required content

Eyebrow: `گروه بین‌المللی مهام`

H1: `برای تصمیم‌های مهم، ابتدا مسیر مناسب خود را بشناسید.`

Supporting text: `مهام به افراد و سازمان‌ها کمک می‌کند با شناخت دقیق‌تر شرایط، اهداف و ظرفیت‌ها، مسیرهای قابل بررسی خود را آگاهانه‌تر ارزیابی کنند.`

Primary CTA: `شروع ارزیابی مسیر`

Secondary CTA: `مهام چگونه کار می‌کند؟`

## Visual composition

Create a dark, atmospheric hero that occupies most of the initial viewport. Use a right-side or background abstract “decision landscape”: layered translucent planes, subtle directional lines, depth, and a luminous horizon. On the complementary side, show two or three intelligent floating information panels.

The panels may contain concepts such as `هدف`, `شرایط فعلی`, `اولویت‌ها`, and `قدم بعدی`. They must feel like elements of a considered framework, not app dashboard widgets.

## Hero rules

- Headline is the primary focal point.
- Use exactly one primary and one secondary CTA.
- Do not use a full-width stock photo behind text.
- Do not make the hero resemble a finance dashboard, travel product, or AI software launch.
- Use gentle ambient motion: drifting light, a slow path draw, or small panel elevation.
- Ensure all text remains high contrast and readable.

## Mobile translation

Move the abstract system below or behind the text without losing depth. Keep CTA buttons large and stacked where necessary. Do not crop the H1 awkwardly.
