# Mahaam — Final Master Prompt for Stitch

---

You are a world-class Creative Director and senior product designer. Design a high-fidelity, premium homepage and reusable visual system for **Mahaam International Group**, an international decision-support organization for professionals, executives, business owners, and organizations.

This is a visual-design and UX task only. Do not generate code.

The website is Persian (Farsi), fully RTL, and right aligned. Use `font-family: "IRANSans", Tahoma, sans-serif`. The interface must feel bespoke, modern, editorial, internationally credible, and exceptionally polished.

## Brand and positioning

Mahaam helps people and organizations understand their circumstances, goals, capabilities, and relevant international pathways. It helps users progress from uncertainty to a more informed next step. Mahaam is not an immigration agency, travel agency, visa seller, recruitment agency, or generic marketplace. Never use promises, urgency, discounts, guarantees, or immigration/travel clichés.

The design should convey calm confidence, discretion, intelligence, structure, and human judgment. Aim for the level of craft associated with leading international digital product studios and premium consulting brands, without copying any company’s design.

## Art direction

Create a refined visual world based on subtle paths, coordinates, layered planes, horizons, and information surfaces. These are abstract metaphors for orientation and decision-making—not literal maps, planes, passports, flags, globes, or visa imagery.

Use a composed palette: midnight ink and navy, deep teal, restrained indigo accent, warm ivory, and cool neutral grays. Build depth with tonal fields, soft mesh gradients, fine grain, hairline grids, understated borders, gentle ambient light, and controlled shadows. Do not leave backgrounds empty, but keep all patterns subtle enough to preserve reading clarity.

Use replaceable placeholder assets for logo, contact details, and images. Photography, where used, must be editorial/documentary: professionals doing meaningful work, contemporary architecture, workshops, and international professional contexts. Never use generic handshakes, airports, tourists, staged smiles, or luxury clichés.

## Layout and typography

Use a 12-column desktop grid with generous space, clear hierarchy, controlled asymmetry, and a maximum content width around 1320px. Build a responsive mobile translation that preserves the hierarchy rather than simply shrinking the desktop layout.

Typography must be elegant and highly readable in Persian. Right align all prose. Never center long paragraphs. Use large, carefully balanced headlines and comfortable body line-height. Make buttons and form elements clear and tactile, with accessible contrast and focus states.

## Homepage content and sequence

Design a coherent narrative in the following order.

### 1. Header

Integrated transparent header over the hero, becoming a compact solid sticky header on scroll.

Logo placeholder: `مهام`

Navigation: `خانه` / `مخاطبان` / `مسیرها` / `ارزیابی مسیر` / `مهام چگونه کار می‌کند؟` / `درباره مهام`

Primary CTA: `شروع ارزیابی مسیر`

### 2. Hero

Eyebrow: `گروه بین‌المللی مهام`

Headline: `برای تصمیم‌های مهم، ابتدا مسیر مناسب خود را بشناسید.`

Text: `مهام به افراد و سازمان‌ها کمک می‌کند با شناخت دقیق‌تر شرایط، اهداف و ظرفیت‌ها، مسیرهای قابل بررسی خود را آگاهانه‌تر ارزیابی کنند.`

Primary CTA: `شروع ارزیابی مسیر`

Secondary CTA: `مهام چگونه کار می‌کند؟`

Make this a dark, atmospheric composition. Include a distinctive abstract “decision landscape” with subtle routes, layers, and ambient horizon light. Include two or three softly elevated information panels with concepts such as `هدف`, `شرایط فعلی`, `اولویت‌ها`, and `قدم بعدی`. These must feel like a thoughtful framework—not SaaS dashboard widgets.

### 3. Audience recognition

Heading: `شما در کدام نقطه از مسیر قرار دارید؟`

Show the four audiences: `متخصصان` / `مدیران` / `صاحبان کسب‌وکار` / `سازمان‌ها`.

Do not use a repetitive four-icon card grid. Use an editorial route system with related but distinct surfaces, visual anchors, and hover potential.

### 4. Mahaam approach

Heading: `هر مسیر، از شناخت دقیق‌تر شروع می‌شود.`

Visualize this sequence elegantly: `شناخت شرایط → روشن‌شدن هدف → بررسی گزینه‌ها → انتخاب قدم بعدی`.

Create a refined timeline, layered diagram, or route system—not a basic numbered list.

### 5. Journey explorer

Heading: `مسیر مناسب، یک پاسخ آماده نیست.`

Feature: `کشف فرصت‌ها` / `تجربه‌های بین‌المللی` / `استقرار و توسعه` / `رشد و همکاری`.

Each route needs a restrained but distinct abstract identity while remaining inside one visual system.

### 6. Assessment teaser

Heading: `پیش از انتخاب، معیارهای درست را بشناسید.`

Show an intelligent, human-centered decision-assessment preview using `هدف`, `شرایط فعلی`, `تجربه و ظرفیت‌ها`, and `اولویت‌ها` as inputs. It must feel transparent and clear, never like a cold scoring algorithm.

CTA: `ارزیابی مسیر من`

### 7. Process

Heading: `گفت‌وگو، بعد از شناخت اولیه معنا پیدا می‌کند.`

Show: `شناخت اولیه` / `بررسی شرایط` / `گفت‌وگوی تخصصی در صورت نیاز` / `طراحی مسیر اختصاصی`.

The design should communicate order and transparency.

### 8. Principles

Heading: `رویکردی مبتنی بر دقت، تناسب و شفافیت`

Use concise, credible principles. Do not add invented numbers, partner logos, testimonials, awards, or claims.

### 9. Closing invitation

Headline: `آماده‌اید مسیر خود را دقیق‌تر بررسی کنید؟`

Text: `با چند پرسش کوتاه، نقطه شروع مناسب‌تری برای بررسی شرایط و هدف خود پیدا کنید.`

CTA: `شروع ارزیابی اولیه`

Make this a calm and elevated conclusion, not a sales banner.

### 10. Footer

Create a spacious, premium dark footer with placeholder logo, short description, columns for Mahaam, audiences, pathways, resources, placeholder contact details, legal links, and understated social icons.

## Components and motion

Design consistent primary, secondary, outline, and text buttons; navigation; cards; assessment controls; inputs; accordions; and feedback states. Cards use architectural radius, 1px borders, subtle cool shadows, and modest hover elevation. Do not overuse pills or glass panels.

Motion should be subtle, expensive, and meaningful: short card lift, navigation glide, section reveal, path drawing, smooth accordion behavior, and gentle hero ambient movement. No bouncing, spinning, flashy particles, excessive parallax, autoplay carousels, or motion that delays content. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Quality bar and prohibitions

The final design must not look AI-generated, template-based, generic, flat, overly colorful, or like a SaaS marketing page. Do not use random gradients, repeated component patterns, empty backgrounds, fake social proof, travel imagery, or aggressive sales language.

Deliver a cohesive high-fidelity desktop homepage concept, clear mobile adaptation intent, and a reusable visual system. Every section must feel intentionally art-directed while the whole page feels like one calm, premium narrative.
