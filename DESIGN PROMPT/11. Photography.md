# Mahaam — Photography Direction

## Role

Photography provides human credibility and real-world texture. It is supporting evidence, not wallpaper.

## Subject matter

- Professionals in focused, authentic work contexts
- Small workshops, meetings, research, discussion, or preparation
- Contemporary workspaces and architecture
- International professional environments without tourist cues
- Details of materials, cities, institutions, or spaces when they imply context rather than spectacle

## Visual qualities

- Editorial and documentary, not commercial stock
- Natural or soft directional light
- Considered framing and occasional cinematic crop
- Calm expressions, purposeful activity, plausible environments
- Muted, color-graded palette compatible with navy, ivory, and teal

## Never use

- Airplanes, airports, luggage, passport closeups, visas, flags
- Fake handshakes, pointing-at-screen teams, call-center headsets
- Perfectly posed people looking at camera without purpose
- Luxury clichés: yachts, towers, private jets, cash
- Overly saturated travel photography

## Implementation

Use responsive image crops and provide a meaningful Persian alt-text placeholder. If photographic rights are unknown, label the asset clearly as a temporary placeholder for replacement.
