# Mahaam — Premium Details

## What makes the design feel bespoke

Premium quality comes from restraint and resolution. Each small detail should reinforce intentionality:

- Baseline alignment between Persian type, icons, and buttons
- Quiet 1px separators with carefully tuned opacity
- Shadows with wide blur, low opacity, and subtle cool tint
- Precise card padding and consistent internal rhythm
- Distinct crop logic for every image, rather than generic rectangles
- Considered loading, hover, focus, and disabled states
- A restrained system of indexes, labels, and section anchors

## Signature interactions

- Header changes surface and density elegantly on scroll
- Route cards reveal a secondary line of context on hover
- Assessment inputs make relationships visible without scoring the user publicly
- Links guide the eye in RTL direction with subtle arrow motion
- Dark sections use ambient light that responds gently to pointer position only if it remains unobtrusive

## Microcopy tone

Use calm, direct Persian:

- Prefer: `شناخت مسیر`, `بررسی اولیه`, `ادامه گفت‌وگو`, `مشاهده جزئیات`
- Avoid: `فرصت طلایی`, `همین حالا`, `تضمینی`, `بهترین انتخاب`, `فقط امروز`

## Density calibration

Do not confuse premium with emptiness. A section may be spacious and still contain useful visual cues, labels, or a meaningful pattern. Conversely, never fill space with arbitrary badges, decorative statistics, or duplicate CTAs.

## Asset replacement

All temporary logos, images, map-like surfaces, and contact details must be visibly modular, so the final implementation can replace them without disturbing hierarchy.
