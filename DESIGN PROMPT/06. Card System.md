# Mahaam — Card System

## Philosophy

Cards are containers for decisions and routes, not a default layout solution. Use them only when their surface, grouping, or interaction improves comprehension.

## Shared construction

- Soft architectural radius: 16–24px
- 1px low-contrast border
- Matte or gently translucent background
- Subtle ambient shadow, never black or harsh
- Comfortable internal padding: 24–36px desktop
- Clear focus and hover state

## Card families

### Audience route cards

For: متخصصان، مدیران، صاحبان کسب‌وکار، سازمان‌ها.

Use four related but not identical compositions. Each card has an understated icon / abstract visual, title, one-sentence context, and directional affordance. Avoid a uniform four-up icon-card grid.

### Journey cards

For: کشف فرصت‌ها، تجربه‌های بین‌المللی، استقرار و توسعه، رشد و همکاری.

Use bolder visual identity than audience cards: each route may have a distinct abstract texture or image crop. Preserve one shared structure and typography system.

### Principle cards

Short, quiet, low-emphasis statements. They should support credibility and may sit in an editorial grid rather than traditional card UI.

### Assessment panels

Use larger, more structured panels showing inputs, relationships, or a next-step recommendation. They must feel clear and human, never gamified.

## States

- Default: clear hierarchy, subtle surface depth
- Hover: 2–4px lift, border / light strengthening, directional indicator moves slightly in RTL direction
- Focus: visible high-contrast ring or outline
- Active: controlled pressed state, no exaggerated scale
- Disabled: reduced contrast while retaining legibility

## Prohibitions

- No rainbow card colors
- No identical icon + title + paragraph + button repetition across the page
- No heavy shadows
- No card within card within card without a strong structural reason
