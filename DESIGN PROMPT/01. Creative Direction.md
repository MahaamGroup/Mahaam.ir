# Mahaam — Creative Direction

## Purpose

Create the visual world for Mahaam International Group: a premium Persian decision-support platform for professionals, executives, business owners, and organizations considering international pathways. This is a website for clarity before action—not a service catalogue or sales funnel.

## Brand stance

Mahaam helps users understand their situation, goals, capabilities, and relevant next steps. It is not an immigration agency, travel agency, visa seller, recruitment company, or generic consultancy marketplace. The design must communicate discretion, judgment, structure, and calm confidence.

## Desired emotional sequence

1. **Arrival:** “This feels credible, distinctive, and international.”
2. **Recognition:** “This understands people in a situation like mine.”
3. **Clarity:** “I can make sense of the choices in front of me.”
4. **Readiness:** “A thoughtful first conversation would be worthwhile.”

## Creative proposition

**From uncertainty to an informed direction.**

Use the visual language of routes, layers, coordinates, horizons, networks, and carefully revealed information. These are metaphors, not literal illustrations. The site should feel like a considered editorial experience combined with the precision of a high-end digital product.

## Audience

- Professionals building international experience or career options
- Executives assessing strategic development opportunities
- Business owners exploring expansion, partnerships, or new markets
- Organizations seeking structured international collaboration

They are discerning, time-poor, and cautious of exaggerated claims. They expect privacy, maturity, and substance.

## Design personality

Premium, intelligent, editorial, restrained, contemporary, human, global, layered, and quietly memorable.

The work may take inspiration from the craft level of Apple, Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Notion, and leading international consulting firms, but must not mimic their layouts or visual assets.

## Non-negotiables

- Entire experience is Persian, RTL, and right aligned.
- Typeface: `"IRANSans", Tahoma, sans-serif`.
- Make decisions visible; do not overpromise outcomes.
- Build one cohesive visual world rather than stacking generic sections.
- Use replaceable placeholders for logo, contact data, photos, and partner assets.
- Design for accessibility and responsive implementation from the outset.

## Avoid

- Airplanes, passports, visa stamps, flags, globes, tourist imagery
- Hard-selling, countdowns, discounts, “guaranteed” language
- Startup-SaaS dashboard aesthetics
- Generic stock-business photography and staged handshakes
- Empty white canvases, repetitive card grids, random decoration

## Core homepage narrative

**Understand your position → identify relevant paths → assess criteria → begin a meaningful conversation.**

The final design must make this progression felt visually, not merely stated in copy.
