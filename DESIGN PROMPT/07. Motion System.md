# Mahaam — Motion System

## Principle

Motion should reveal hierarchy, explain change, and make the interface feel carefully crafted. It should never demand attention for its own sake.

## Motion character

Calm, light, responsive, and slightly editorial. Prefer opacity, transform, mask reveal, and background-position changes. Use transforms and opacity for performance.

## Timing

- Micro-feedback: 120–180ms
- Button / card transitions: 180–240ms
- Section reveal: 420–650ms
- Ambient hero movement: 8–18 seconds, very low amplitude

Use ease-out curves for entrance and natural ease-in-out for ambient movement. Avoid bounce, elastic, and spin.

## Approved behaviors

- A small rise and border-light change for cards on hover
- Underline or indicator glide for navigation
- Staggered but restrained reveal of content on initial section entry
- Accordion height and icon rotation with clear state change
- Path lines drawing gently in the hero or journey system
- Subtle light shift behind the CTA on hover
- Smooth sticky-header condensation

## Avoid

- Auto-playing carousels
- Infinite movement on every card
- Parallax that affects readability or causes motion sickness
- Oversized cursor effects
- Text scrambling, bouncing numbers, or flashy particles
- Delayed content that harms scanning

## Accessibility

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: remove ambient loops and replace large transforms with instant or short opacity changes. Motion must never be required to understand content.
