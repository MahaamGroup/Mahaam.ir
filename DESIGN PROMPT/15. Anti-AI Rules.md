# Mahaam — Anti-AI Design Rules

## Primary rule

The result must look art-directed, not assembled from familiar AI landing-page parts.

## Do not do any of the following

1. Use the same card shape, icon placement, and text pattern repeatedly.
2. Put three feature cards directly below a generic hero without narrative purpose.
3. Use gradients as a substitute for art direction.
4. Use floating blobs, neon glows, or glass panels everywhere.
5. Create an all-white background with arbitrary colored blocks.
6. Add fake testimonials, client logos, partner logos, awards, metrics, or press mentions.
7. Use stock photos of handshakes, passports, planes, airports, flags, or smiling people in suits.
8. Present unverified promises, guarantees, urgency, scarcity, or sales slogans.
9. Make every CTA identical or place a CTA in every section.
10. Fill headings with buzzwords such as “transform”, “unlock”, “revolutionary”, or “seamless”.
11. Use a dashboard visual language for the decision assessment.
12. Overuse rounded pills, oversized gradients, and 3D emoji-like icons.
13. Center long Persian paragraphs or break RTL alignment.
14. Hide the site’s structure under excessive animation.
15. Invent services, countries, offices, numbers, certifications, or claims.

## Required countermeasures

- Create visual rhythm by varying composition while retaining design tokens.
- Use authentic hierarchy, section-specific art direction, and editable placeholders.
- Keep the brand’s decision-support positioning visible in copy and visuals.
- Prioritize clarity, accessibility, and Persian readability over visual spectacle.
- Every decorative element must support atmosphere, orientation, or hierarchy.
